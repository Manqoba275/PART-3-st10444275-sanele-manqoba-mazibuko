Process

From idea to launch with a clear business-focused workflow.

The process keeps the project simple to follow: understand the business, plan the pages, design the journey, build the site, test properly, launch, and support it after delivery.

01

Discover

Understand your business, customers, goals, offer, and the result the website needs to create.

02

Plan

Define pages, features, content, technical requirements, forms, and the right customer journey.

03

Design

Create the visual direction, page structure, calls to action, and mobile-first layouts.

04

Build

Develop the website, backend forms, integrations, animations, and responsive behaviour.

05

Test

Check mobile, desktop, forms, links, performance, usability, and content before launch.

06

Launch

Deploy the website, connect key services, and make sure the business can start using it.

07

Support

Maintain, update, improve, and expand the site as the business grows.
